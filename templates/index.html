ℹ️ What is Prenatal Jaundice?

Prenatal (or neonatal) jaundice is a common condition in newborns where the skin and eyes appear yellow due to high levels of bilirubin in the blood. It usually appears within the first few days after birth and often resolves on its own. However, early detection is crucial, as severe cases can lead to complications like brain damage if untreated.

Our system uses advanced AI techniques to analyze baby images and predict signs of jaundice, assisting in early diagnosis and timely medical attention.
